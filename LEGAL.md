# Responsible Use

MoonTrace is a self-hosted media fetcher intended for personal archiving,
research, and content that the user owns or is authorized to access.

## Access boundaries

MoonTrace is designed to use only the permissions already available to the
current user/account. It does not provide features intended to bypass DRM,
paywalls, membership restrictions, purchase checks, or other access controls.

Browser login support reuses the user's existing local browser session.
MoonTrace stores the selected browser/profile configuration; it does not store
the user's Bilibili password or export browser cookies into the project config.

## Copyright and platform rules

Downloading a work does not automatically grant permission to republish,
redistribute, sell, or otherwise exploit that work.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable copyright law and the
terms of service of the platforms they use with MoonTrace.

Recommended use includes:
- media you created or uploaded yourself;
- media whose rightsholder has authorized you to save;
- content explicitly provided for download or archival use;
- other media you are legally authorized to access and use.

This document describes the intended design and use boundaries of MoonTrace.
It is not legal advice for any particular jurisdiction or situation.
